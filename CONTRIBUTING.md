Dictately is licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You may use, copy, fork, modify, redistribute, and sell your own version, including for commercial use. Keep the copyright and MIT permission notice with copies or substantial portions. Dependencies and model weights retain their own [licenses](THIRD_PARTY.md).

## Work in your own copy

1. Click **Fork** on GitHub to create a copy under your account, or clone/download the source
2. Create a branch in your fork and make your changes there
3. Follow [Development and testing](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) to build and check your version
4. You may distribute your fork under the MIT terms, or optionally submit a pull request for review

The official repository is maintained by its owner. Forking and the MIT license do not grant push or merge access here. Changes to the official repository require the maintainer's review and approval; pull requests are not guaranteed to be accepted. Issues and pull requests must not include personal recordings, transcript history, credentials, or downloaded model weights.
